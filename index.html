Write prompts that get the answer you wanted.

A prompt is the instruction you give an AI assistant. Prompt engineering is the habit of writing those instructions clearly. CO-STAR is a simple six-part checklist that helps you do it every time.

  • Context + Objective + Style
  • Tone + Audience + Response
  • Better control over output

The CO-STAR framework

  • CContextBackground for the task
  • OObjectiveExactly what you want done
  • SStyleHow it should be written
  • TToneEmotional / professional feel
  • AAudienceWho will read the output
  • RResponseFormat, length, structure

The CO-STAR guide

Each element answers one question. Answer all six and your prompt leaves little room for guesswork.

Context

What it is: The background the AI needs to understand your situation.

Ask yourself: What does the AI need to know first?

Example: I manage IT for a 40-person office and we are moving email to a new provider next month.

Objective

What it is: The specific task you want done.

Ask yourself: What exactly should the AI produce?

Example: Write a checklist of the steps to move every mailbox with no lost messages.

Style

What it is: The writing format or voice, such as a report, a story or a how-to guide.

Ask yourself: What kind of writing do I want?

Example: A clear, step-by-step how-to guide.

Tone

What it is: The attitude of the writing, such as friendly, formal or reassuring.

Ask yourself: How should it feel to read?

Example: Calm and reassuring.

Audience

What it is: The people who will read the result.

Ask yourself: Who is this for, and what do they already know?

Example: Office staff with no technical background.

Response

What it is: The shape of the answer: length, layout or file type.

Ask yourself: How should the answer be delivered?

Example: A numbered list of no more than 10 steps, each under 20 words.
