Write prompts that get the answer you wanted.

A prompt is the instruction you give an AI assistant. Prompt engineering is the practice of writing those instructions precisely, so the results are accurate and usable. CO-STAR is a six-part framework that gives every prompt a clear structure.

  • Context, Objective and Style
  • Tone, Audience and Response
  • Consistent, reliable output

The CO-STAR framework

  • CContextBackground and situation the AI needs
  • OObjectiveThe specific task to be completed
  • SStyleFormat and writing approach
  • TToneAttitude and level of formality
  • AAudienceWho will read and use the output
  • RResponseLength, structure and deliverable format
